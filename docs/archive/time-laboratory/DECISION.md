## Interpretation boundaries

“All possible combinations” means all eight combinations of the defined binary interventions at a selected year. It does not mean every possible climate, strength, timing, stochastic future, or real-world event.

“Quantum” is the fictional name of the branching experiment interface. No quantum computation or probabilities are implied.

“4D” means spatial object histories projected with time into one explorable 3D frame. The user has emphasized object visibility and depth; do not revert to a playback-only feature.
