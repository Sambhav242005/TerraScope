# Lessons and mistakes to avoid

1. **Expanding beyond the requested case.** The user explicitly returned to the original plan. Do not reintroduce Quantum, decision trees, 4D or 3D terrain.
2. **Showing the answer before investigation.** Let observations support reasoning; reserve the causal verdict until after experimentation.
3. **A rescue animation without causality.** World, chart and verdict must consume the same model output. Test all interventions.
4. **Unfair counterfactuals.** Shared climate and history must not depend on random-call order or which experiment ran first.
5. **Assuming feeding always helps.** A short supplement given too early can expire before starvation. Show actual outcome and timing.
6. **Claiming prototype science is validated.** This is an authored fictional ecological model; its internal consistency can be tested, real-world validity has not been established.
7. **Claiming delivery on unverified competition dates.** Verify current rules separately before publishing/submission.
