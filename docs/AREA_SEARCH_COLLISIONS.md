# Area search and collision prevention ? 5 November 2026

> Current status (5 November 2026): [Latest delivery, verification and remaining gaps](CURRENT_STATUS.md) supersedes older implementation-status statements below. Earlier dated notes are retained as history. R01-R18 remain active.


Partial R01/R03/R06/R11/R13/R15/R16. New placements now pass an admission check before being persisted or drawn. Building, road/rail centerline, channel/water and existing-proposal overlaps are rejected with the matched explanation. Existing natural-storage candidate adoption can retain water overlap as investigation; roads, buildings and other-proposal overlaps still block it. Missing evidence and footprints outside loaded OSM coverage block placement until the area is selected and loaded. None of this certifies suitability.

Legacy saved proposals are preserved; their conflicts are rechecked against map evidence and other proposals. Conflicting or unchecked 3D models are hidden, while their selectable pins, explanations and deletion remain available. This resolves the previous warning-only behavior that still rendered solar tables through houses. The solar envelope contains the rendered table group. The wind screening envelope now includes at least the illustrative 66 m rotor span, not only its smaller assumed foundation area. No regulatory setback, measured road width, underground service coverage or engineering clearance is inferred. Decorative trees are not observed tree positions and are not treated as surveyed obstacles.

Place search uses explicitly submitted Photon queries, no autocomplete, five results, 15-second timeout, a minimum 1.1-second interval and a 20-query memory cache. VITE_GEOCODER_URL can replace the default Photon-compatible endpoint. Attribution/terms are shown. Official API/policy: https://github.com/komoot/photon . The public demo permits reasonable use but has no availability guarantee; production requires an appropriate provider or private instance. Nominatim public search is not used. Provider failures preserve the selected area and offer coordinate selection/retry.

Selecting a search result, area coordinates or blank map space in Select mode updates the shared analysis centre, resets stale terrain results, moves the camera and starts supported terrain screening. Panning alone makes no regional data request. Selected area/name are saved locally and restored. Reset view returns to that area. Sonpur camera bookmarks are disabled while a searched area is selected to prevent mismatched labels. Use search/coordinates to select another region.

A selected area outside the bounded archived Sonpur extract triggers a bounded approximately 4 km-wide OSM v0.6 map JSON request. Nodes and complete ways supply building polygons, road/rail lines and water/channel features; multipolygon relations and incomplete ways remain omitted and disclosed. Retrieval time, query URL, SHA-256 and geographic WGS84 query bounds accompany the source. Requests have a 15-second deadline and a 12 MB parsed-response guard. This is an in-page connector, not a durable background service or guaranteed worldwide coverage. Returned complete ways may extend beyond query bounds; positive intersections are retained but no absence/clearance is inferred outside bounds. Required official source/API documentation: https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/API_v0.6 . ODbL attribution retained.

Historical rainfall, discharge, facilities and NRSC snapshots remain the original Sonpur development case. The selected-area banner and map heading explicitly distinguish them; searching Bengaluru does not produce Bengaluru historical hydrology or official Indian flood coverage. OSM geographic context/search is distinct from NRSC scientific evidence. Other terrain/data/scientific prerequisites and R01?R18 scope remain active.
