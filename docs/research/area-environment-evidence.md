# Area environment evidence — 17 November 2026

R19–R21 extend, and do not replace, R01–R18. Sonpur/Bihar is provisional. These are current research findings and a bounded connector, not validated scientific predictions.

## Evidence distinctions

`src/flood/areaReview/contracts.ts` separates documented corridor, habitat, sighting, forest survey report, historical observed flood footprint, modeled flood susceptibility, air monitor observation and modeled pollution. Historical flood atlas is a separate ninth kind because a frequency atlas is not an event footprint. Every record carries source/provider/version, retrieval and valid dates, issue time or explicit null, bounds with meaning, resolution, units, license, missing coverage, uncertainty and status. A report-only record does not establish spatial overlap. Search envelopes are never emitted as geometry. No sensitive wildlife point, inferred route or traced report-map polygon is stored.

* [Open-Meteo air API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api), [terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms): actual public JSON request passed for 25.7N, 85.18E on 17 November 2026. Data license CC BY 4.0, CAMS/Open-Meteo attribution required. Free service is non-commercial, limited to fewer than 10,000 requests/day per terms; commercial hosting needs an appropriate subscription/proxy. No API key used. No health recommendations or pollution source attribution generated.

## Functioning behavior and remaining gaps

Area coordinates automatically select the curated Bihar report catalog and trigger one debounced, abortable air-model request. This is deterministic source lookup, not a local AI web-research agent. It runs only while mounted. Outside the rough discovery envelope reports are absent; inside it regional relevance does not establish administrative membership or local evidence coverage. Air is worldwide model context independent of historical playback, with unavailable/stale/missing distinctions. Upstream model-run/issue-time is not supplied; not suitable for retrospective forecast-skill testing. No local precision or quantified confidence is inferred.

No corridor/habitat/sighting geometries, event-level observed flood polygons, modeled flood susceptibility, live CPCB observations, water pollution or soil pollution have been connected. Those require separate lawful data access, spatial coverage and scientific interpretation. These are recorded dependencies, not cancelled scope.
